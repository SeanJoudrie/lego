# Piece-by-piece breakdown

Each figure's photo sliced into regions and identified separately.
Guesses, not facts — confirm before trusting.


49 photos, 105 pieces identified, 11 photos where no figure could be located

## What it settled

**The Mechanic's legs — the ones you'd forgotten.** `970c00pb0021`, *Hips and
Legs with Space UFO Gold Circuitry and Silver Belt Pattern*, 76%. Gold circuitry
legs on the character who modified his own arm in gold.

**Armstrong and the Deputy wear the same torso.** Both come back
`Torso SW Vest Open over Sand Blue Shirt` at 89% and 91% — the highest-agreement
pair in the collection. In the lore that torso is the Royal Intelligence Agency
uniform, which is now confirmed at the part level and not just by eye.

**The Senate coat is literally shared plastic.** The Prince's Senate-era torso
and both photos of the Royal Daughter return the same part, 80–89%. She really
is wearing her father's jacket.

**Insectoids holds up piece by piece.** The Second is an Insectoids Droid torso,
the Engineer an Insectoids Silver X, the Twins an Insectoids Blue X. Three
characters, one 1998 theme.

**The Discarded and the Mad Ticket Man are both Indiana Jones parts** — an
Indiana Jones head on one, an Indiana Jones vest-and-bow-tie torso on the other.
In the lore, those two come from the same world. They come from the same LEGO
theme as well.

**The First Man is a train worker.** `Torso Train Blue Striped Overalls`, 88%.
The figure the whole world is built around came off a LEGO train set.

Also: Heavy wears Senate Commando armour, and the royal line began in the
Senate. The Palace Chief is Rock Raiders. The repair crew chief is Exo-Force,
which is where that AT.01 print comes from. The Samurai is Castle Ninja.
